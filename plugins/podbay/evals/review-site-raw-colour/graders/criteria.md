Check the review report for these three things. Pass only when all three are true.
1. It reports the new `<style>` block (the `.banner` rule with `#ff6a00` and raw px values) in site/index.html as a finding with severity "should fix" or "blocker".
2. It says site pages should take their parts from `site/site.css` or `site/styleguide.html`.
3. It says to use an existing part, or to have the design-system owner add one first.
Skipped steps, notes about missing files, and the tier the report states do not matter.
